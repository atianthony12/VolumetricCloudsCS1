[h1]How to use[/h1]
[olist]
[*][b]Subscribe[/b] to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2040656402]Harmony[/url] and then this mod, make sure both are enabled. If you use Skyve, make sure the mod is enabled in it.
[*][b]Load a city[/b] (new or saved) and the volumetric weather should appear. The mod does not run in the editors or in scenarios.
[*][b]Press F4[/b] (or click the mod's button) to open the in-game panel.
[*][b]Press Shift+F4[/b] to toggle the clouds/fog (useful when building, detailing, etc.). Both keys can be changed in the mod's options.
[/olist]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Features[/h1]
[h3]Clouds[/h3]
[list]
[*]Raymarched volumetric clouds that drift with the wind and cast their shadows on the city
[*]Towering cumulus, soft cotton puffs and flat overcast layers, mixed in one sky and changing as the clouds build up
[*]Sunlight that glows through thin edges, darker storm bases and red sunsets lighting the clouds from below
[*]Clouds that follow the curve of the Earth all the way to the horizon
[*]Distant clouds from HDRI skies by [url=https://polyhaven.com]Poly Haven[/url]
[/list]
[h3]Weather[/h3]
[list]
[*]A complete weather overhaul that follows the game's weather, or lets you set your own
[*]Rain that falls only under the clouds that carry it, slanting and gusting with the wind
[*]Thunderstorms that travel across the map, with lightning striking the ground and crawling through the clouds
[*]Wet roads, puddles and spray from car wheels
[*]Volumetric fog: morning fog, mist after rain, fog filling valleys and lying over water, lit by the city's lights
[*]Rainbows and waterspouts
[*]Enhanced trees movement in the wind (experimental and off by default in the Weather tab)
[*]BONUS: a fix for the lights in the game (Halos tab)
[/list]
[h3]Mod options[/h3]
[list]
[*]Separate settings for day, sunset, night and overcast skies
[*]Profiles to save and switch between your favorite settings
[*]4 in-game panel looks
[*]Localization: English, Spanish, French and Simplified Chinese
[/list]

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
Tested together with [b]AD Cloud Replacer[/b], [b]Dynamic Cubemap[/b], [b]Render It![/b], [b]Theme Mixer[/b], [b]Play It![/b], [b]Fog Controller[/b] and more, with no conflicts.
Works on Windows, Mac and Linux as of v1.1.1.
[b]Removing the mod[/b] should not corrupt your saves.

[h1]Reporting a bug[/h1]
Please include the following:
[olist]
[*][b]The game's log[/b], [i]output_log.txt[/i]. In Steam, right-click Cities: Skylines → [i]Manage → Browse local files[/i], then open the [i]Cities_Data[/i] folder. On a Mac it is [i]~/Library/Logs/Unity/Player.log[/i], on Linux [i]~/.config/unity3d/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines/Player.log[/i].
[*][b]The mod's own log[/b], [i]VolumetricClouds.log[/i]. Tick [i]Detailed logging[/i] (Options → Volumetric Weather) first, then make the problem happen again. To find it, paste this into File Explorer's address bar:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines[/code]
On a Mac it is in [i]~/Library/Application Support/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines[/i], on Linux in [i]~/.local/share/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines[/i].
[*][b]What happened[/b]: what you did and expected, screenshots, your graphics card and your other mods.
[/olist]
Both logs start fresh at every launch, so copy them before restarting, upload them (Google Drive, Pastebin...) and post the link in the comments.
Or [url=https://github.com/atianthony12/VolumetricCloudsCS1/issues]open an issue on GitHub[/url] instead, where you can attach the log files directly.
