[h1]Version 1.6.0[/h1]
[b]New[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Cloud variety[/b]: some stretches of sky grow tall towering cumulus, others stay flat and small (Clouds tab).
[*][b]Softer cumulus[/b]: the heaped clouds get soft, wispy edges instead of hard blocks (Clouds tab).
[*][b]Rounded cumulus bases[/b]: cumulus bottoms are rounded and uneven instead of one flat line (Clouds tab).
[*][b]Natural Classic clouds[/b]: Classic clouds get thinner toward their edges and rounded, instead of flat slabs (Clouds tab).
[*][b]Wind on the rain[/b] and [b]Finer rain[/b]: rain leans with the wind, with drops of many sizes, sheets of heavier rain, splashes, rain lit by street lights and no rain under roofs and bridges.
[*][b]Wet roads and puddles[/b]: roads get dark and shiny in the rain and mirror the city, with puddles; grass and trees have their own sliders.
[*][b]Spray from wheels[/b] and [b]Storm mist[/b].
[*][b]Travelling storms[/b] and [b]Lightning variety[/b]: storms come in from upwind and move away, with bolts of many shapes and colors.
[*][b]Flare around lights[/b]: lamps throw thin rays of light, with the replacement light halos (Halos tab).
[*][b]New mod defaults[/b]: a new sky for new installs. Existing players keep theirs and get a one-time message offering to try them.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*]The in-game panel now always shows every tab, Weather and Halos included: [i]Show advanced options[/i] is gone.
[*][i]Smoother clouds[/i] is much faster on 4K screens and with Dynamic Resolution over 100%, and no longer smears when the camera moves.
[*]The clouds draw faster for everyone, with the same look.
[*]Soft clouds under [i]Mixed[/i] and [i]Auto[/i] are now as bright as the heaped clouds beside them.
[*][i]Storms[/i] and [i]Cloud position[/i] moved to the Now tab.
[*]New installs start on the Medium quality preset (or Low on weaker computers). Existing players keep their quality.
[/list]
[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*]Rain curtains no longer light up like day when [i]Cloud brightness[/i] is raised at night.
[*]A lightning bolt no longer leaves dark lines in the rain and clouds behind it.
[*]A long profile name with an emoji could stop the game from drawing new text.
[/list]
