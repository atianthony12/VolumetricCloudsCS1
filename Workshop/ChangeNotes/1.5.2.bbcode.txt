[h1]Version 1.5.2[/h1]
[b]New[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Curved clouds[/b]: the cloud layer follows the curve of the Earth and carries on down to the horizon, with no repeating pattern far out (Weather tab; a trial, off by default).
[*][b]Glare around near lamps[/b]: with the replacement light halos on, lamps near the camera get a soft glow that grows in fog and rain (Halos tab).
[*][b]Trees move in the wind[/b]: trees lean and sway with the weather's wind, gusts roll across forests, and strong wind blows leaves and dust about (Weather tab; experimental, off by default).
[*][b]Wind strength[/b]: set the city's wind by hand, from calm to a gale (Now tab). Other mods can read the wind too; Real Flag's flags fly in it.
[*][b]Simpler clouds for older graphics cards[/b]: a lighter cloud shader, picked by the mod by itself where the full one doesn't draw, or ticked on the options page.
[*][b]Where to show mod icon[/b]: Unified UI, the mod's own button, or none (options page).
[/list]
[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*]On some graphics cards (a 2017 iMac among them) the clouds vanished without any message since 1.5.0; the mod now checks that its clouds really draw and falls back to the simpler ones.
[*]The mod's button shows its name when the mouse rests on it.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*]Rain curtains are lit by the sun only where it gets in under the clouds.
[*]Under Real Light's sky, no more dark disc round a low sun in the far clouds.
[*]A new white icon on a round button, matching Play It!'s and Render It's.
[*][i]Dynamic distant clouds[/i] is off for new installs; players who have it on keep it.
[/list]
