[h1]Version 1.5.2[/h1]
[b]New[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Curved clouds[/b]: clouds follow the curve of the Earth all the way to the horizon, without the repeating pattern in the distance (Weather tab, experimental, off by default).
[*][b]Glare around near lamps[/b]: lamps close to the camera get a soft glow, stronger in fog and rain (Halos tab, needs the light halos on).
[*][b]Trees move in the wind[/b]: trees sway with the wind, gusts move across forests, and strong wind blows leaves and dust around (Weather tab, experimental, off by default).
[*][b]Wind strength[/b]: set the wind yourself, from calm to a gale (Now tab).
[*][b]Simpler clouds for older graphics cards[/b]: a lighter version of the clouds, used automatically when the full ones don't work on your graphics card. You can also turn it on in the mod's options.
[*][b]Where to show mod icon[/b]: Unified UI, the toolbar, or never (mod options).
[/list]
[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*]Clouds not showing on some graphics cards since 1.5.0, like older Macs. The mod now checks that the clouds actually show, and switches to the simpler clouds if they don't.
[*]The mod's button now shows its name when hovered.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*]Rain curtains are only lit by the sun where it shines in under the clouds.
[*]New white icon on a round button, matching Play It! and Render It!.
[*][i]Dynamic distant clouds[/i] is now off by default for new installs. If you already have it on, it stays on.
[/list]
